      1. Add screenshots of each screen — drag & drop them here, paste one with Ctrl+V, or use “+ Add screenshots” on the left.
      2. Mark them up with arrows, boxes and blur, and write what the reader should do.
      3. Export a web guide or PDF to share.
      Screenshots auto-order by file date — drag the cards on the left to reorder.
